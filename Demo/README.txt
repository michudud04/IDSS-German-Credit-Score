Place the 3-5 minute demo video (e.g. demo.mp4) in this folder before creating the final PW3 deliverable ZIP.

Suggested flow for the 3-5 minute video:
1. Overview of the Credit Decision Support System and the two target users (Bank Loan Officer and Customer).
2. Case 1 (Low-risk applicant): automatic approval, model agreement, key factors, and similar past applicants.
3. Case 2 (Borderline applicant): grey-zone review, moving the risk cut-off / margin sliders, and actionable customer advice.
4. Case 3 (High-risk applicant): rejection due to high predicted default risk and serious late payments, plus the customer letter.
5. Case 4 (Unusual profile): out-of-distribution warning (triggers and reasons) preventing automatic approval when models extrapolate.
6. Models tab: ensemble weights, logistic regression weights, and XGBoost feature importances.
